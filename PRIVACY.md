# YourMail Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-09

YourMail is a desktop email client for Windows that works with Gmail. It is built and used by its developer, Alex ("gokawa"). This page explains what data the app touches and what happens to it.

## What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, YourMail asks for:

- Full access to your Gmail mailbox (`https://mail.google.com/`). This is needed to read, search, label, archive, delete and send mail, and to empty Trash and Spam permanently.
- Your Google account email address (`userinfo.email`), to show which account is signed in.

## Where the data is stored

Everything stays on your own computer.

- Mail that YourMail downloads is kept in an encrypted local database on your PC.
- Your Google sign-in tokens are stored locally on your PC.
- YourMail has no server of its own. The developer does not receive, store or see your mail, your tokens or your address.

## What YourMail does not do

- It does not sell, share or transfer your Google user data to anyone.
- It does not show ads and does not use analytics or tracking.
- It does not use your data to train AI or machine-learning models.
- It uses your Google data only to provide the mail features you see in the app.

## Third parties the app talks to

- **Google (Gmail API):** to fetch and change your mail, as you instruct.
- **Google Translate:** only when you ask YourMail to translate a message. The text of that message is sent to Google Translate for that request.
- **Websites that host images in an email:** remote images are blocked by default. They are loaded only if you choose to load them, and then your computer contacts the image host directly.

## Compliance statement

YourMail's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Deleting your data

- Signing out of an account in YourMail deletes its stored sign-in tokens.
- Deleting the `YourMail` folder in `%LOCALAPPDATA%` removes the local mail database and settings.
- You can revoke YourMail's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
